2 How the Live ATS Match Gauge Works

Enterprise ATS systems (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, iCIMS) rank candidates based on keyword coverage and competency density. ResumeWatcher calculates a weighted match percentage:

45%

Core Competencies

Direct matches against high-stakes industry skills defined in the persona.

40%

Job Posting Terms

Frequency-weighted keywords extracted from the employer's exact posting.

15%

Action Verb Strength

High-impact verbs that replace weak phrases like "responsible for".

💡 Pro-tip: Look at the Critical Gaps to Bridge tags. When you click "Tailor Bullets", Gemini naturally weaves these missing competencies into your achievement stories while preserving factual accuracy.

3 STAR Methodology for Resume Bullets

Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds skimming a resume. Passive duty lists get rejected; outcome-driven bullet points get interviews.

Weak Duty

"Responsible for handling media inquiries and writing press releases during company events."

STAR Rewrite

Formulated rapid response messaging and executive holding statements during 4 critical brand incidents (Situation/Task), coordinating cross-functional legal briefings (Action) to mitigate negative media coverage by 65% (Result).

4 AP-Style Cover Letter Strategy

  • No generic fluff: Eliminate clichés like "I am writing to express my eager interest in your company."
  • Journalistic hook: The first sentence immediately states your core capability and direct relevance to their current growth or crisis point.
  • The Proof Paragraph: 2 concrete achievements from your resume demonstrating measurable business value.
  • Forward Momentum Closing: Clear, confident call-to-action requesting a strategic discussion.

Privacy & Security: Your documents are parsed entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript (pdf.js and mammoth.js). No resumes are ever stored on a database or saved to our servers.
